# MUSITU Phase 03 — Private International Growth and Qualified Enquiry Standard

**Owner authorization:** 8 November 2026. This approval covers aggregated search visibility and qualified enquiry reporting, not disclosure of individual correspondence.

## Approved processing

1. Separate Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster by provider, day and normalized ISO country code. Preserve source-reported clicks, impressions and average positions. Never blend average positions across search engines, infer index coverage from submitted sitemap counts, or invent a zero where no provider observation exists.
2. Exclude exact search queries and customer identifiers from all dashboard exports. Internal keyword themes must be broad classifications. Suppress email addresses and other identifying terms entirely.
3. A qualified enquiry is one distinct voluntary business-relevant enquiry about a publicly offered MUSITU solution, excluding spam, bots, duplicates and unsolicited promotions. Confirm lawful notice and consent as applicable. Count repeat replies to a single enquiry thread only once.
4. Qualified counts are UTC-day aggregate totals from an authorized, individually reviewed mailbox/CRM tally and explicitly declared permitted channels. Do not copy messages, names, emails, IPs, query text or user-agent data; never infer country-level conversion from Google country impressions.
5. An absent source is **UNAVAILABLE, not zero**. Do not claim a live conversion channel, Bing reporting or indexing outcome until authenticated readback exists.
6. Keep reports owner-private and outside the site deployment. No website tracker, browser fingerprint, cookie, personal targeting or external script is permitted as a side effect of this work. Establish retention against verified Privacy Policy and actual regulatory obligations, never a fabricated statutory period.
7. Use the validated private CSV normalizer in scripts/international_growth_report.py and the offline HTML renderer in scripts/render_growth_dashboard.py. Synthetic privacy tests enforce source and aggregate shape; real inputs remain private and outside Git.
