## 3. Current on-branch Privacy Policy — sections for markup (not legal approved)

### P1. Scope

This notice applies to browsing the MUSITU corporate website, using its locally operated discovery features, and contacting MUSITU through the listed support email. Separately hosted applications and payment services have their own applicable notices.

### P2. Website visits

Browsing corporate information pages and using on-page product discovery do not require a visitor account. Website hosting and security services may process request metadata such as IP address, browser type, requested page, timestamp and security events to deliver and protect the site.

### P3. Browser-local features

The theme setting uses session storage in the browser. Site search, product filters, the Navigator and the Ecosystem Atlas use public information within the browser; these interactions do not send their queries to an AI model.

### P4. AI features

The optional Ask MUSITU live AI service is not active on this website. A browser-local corporate information guide may help navigate public content. If live AI services are introduced, the applicable notice will be updated before personal information is submitted to a model.

### P5. Contact and correspondence

Messages sent to support@mftintelligence.com may include the sender’s email address, message content and relevant delivery metadata. Correspondence is used to receive, route and respond to enquiries, maintain support records and protect the support channel. Do not send passwords, one-time codes, payment credentials or unnecessary sensitive records.

### P6. Other MUSITU experiences

Linked products may have different data practices and their own notices. For example, MUSITU Intelligence offers browser-local document workflows and separately controlled workspace options, while Chemistry Mastery provides its own product and purchase information.

### P7. Service providers and retention

Cloudflare supports website delivery, security and email routing. Google-hosted mailbox services receive support correspondence, and Resend is used for approved outgoing support messages. Service providers may retain technical and delivery information under their service settings. Information is retained only as needed for appropriate operations, correspondence, security and applicable obligations; specific retention practices are governed by the operational policies of the relevant service.

### P8. Privacy questions and requests

Depending on applicable law, individuals may request information about their personal data or seek access, correction, objection or deletion. Please contact support@mftintelligence.com and provide only the information needed to identify the enquiry.

### P9. International processing and updates

Infrastructure and communication services can process information in more than one jurisdiction. Relevant legal safeguards apply to any cross-border processing. This notice may be updated as website functionality or service arrangements change; material changes will be reflected in the published version.

## 4. Current on-branch Terms — sections for markup (not legal approved)

### T1. About this website

MUSITU publishes information about its products, research and examples, alongside links to separate services. Accessing this website does not itself create an account, a product subscription, a license entitlement or a paid service agreement.

### T2. Product readiness

Published releases, demonstrated prototypes and research concepts are different states. A page describing a potential capability is not a promise that it is generally available.

### T3. External product destinations

Some links lead to independently hosted MUSITU or third-party services. The corporate site does not itself create a customer account, license entitlement or enterprise agreement.

### T4. AI and local guidance

The website offers a browser-local guide to public content. Any future live AI function will be clearly identified and governed by separate information and safeguards. Generated or illustrative content does not create contractual commitments or authorize transactions.

### T5. Research and financial information

Quantitative examples and financial market research are informational. They are not individualized investment advice, a guarantee of returns or a live trading service.

### T6. Educational information

Educational tools can support revision but do not guarantee grades or official examination-board endorsement.

### T7. Acceptable use

Do not interfere with systems, circumvent security, attempt unauthorized access or use the website to obtain confidential records.

### T8. Intellectual property

Original material may be protected by applicable rights. Third-party marks and software retain their respective licenses. Public access does not automatically grant unrestricted redistribution permission.

### T9. Availability, accuracy and updates

Content and services may change; product pages should be revalidated. No uptime guarantee or production service commitment is created merely by describing the website.

### T10. Privacy, enquiries and applicable rights

Our website privacy notice describes relevant handling of website and contact information. Applicable consumer, data-protection and other mandatory rights are not excluded by these website terms. Questions about website use may be sent to support@mftintelligence.com .
