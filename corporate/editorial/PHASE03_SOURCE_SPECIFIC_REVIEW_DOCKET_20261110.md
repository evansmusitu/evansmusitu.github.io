**Release authority: NONE. Publication/indexation: NOT AUTHORIZED.** This is a research and reviewer handoff, not a claim of human fact-checking, legal signoff, financial advice, localization approval or product validation.

## Why this docket exists

The existing twelve owner-approved manuscripts, dated 8 November 2026, are source-hash-locked and should not be silently rewritten. On an independent source-fit audit each had one external background-reference link, with a large concentration on the Federal Reserve SR 26-2. Such broad contextual links are not evidence that a topic-specific quantitative-method claim has been independently checked. This docket identifies more directly relevant **primary or author-hosted references** for a qualified independent reviewer to assess. Do not copy source prose or infer rights to reuse figures. Only the editorial reviewer can decide whether the draft requires additional inline citations, corrections or replacement citations, after which owner approval/source hashes and release tests must be refreshed.

**Source caveats:** Federal Reserve SR 26-2 (17 April 2026) states expected primary relevance to Federal Reserve-regulated banking organizations with more than $30 billion in assets and supersedes SR 11-7. Basel Committee standards often target particular regulated banking entities. Do not present either as globally binding on all MUSITU users or as proof of Axiom's implementation. NIST's AI RMF is voluntary, and the NIST AI RMF Playbook expressly states it is not a universal checklist.

**Verified first-party regulatory scope clarification (13 November 2026):** The Basel Committee's [BCBS Newsletter No 36, published 6 January 2026](https://www.bis.org/publications/implementation-principles-effective-risk-data-aggregation-and-risk-reporting-bcbs-239-principles), expressly states that its summary of implementation themes **does not constitute new supervisory guidance or expectations**. Do not turn that informational newsletter into an alleged new rule for MUSITU or all banks. The [Basel SRP36 risk-data principles](https://www.bis.org/committees/bcbs/basel-framework/standard/srp/36/inforce/2019-12-15/published/2019-12-15) target systemically important banks; for other banks the [RMA10 application summary](https://www.bis.org/committees/bcbs/basel-consolidated-guidelines/module/rma/10) distinguishes guidance and proportionate national application. The [Federal Reserve's SR 26-2](https://www.federalreserve.gov/supervisionreg/srletters/SR2602.htm) is expected to be most relevant to Federal Reserve-regulated banks with over $30 billion in assets, and supersedes both SR 11-7 and SR 21-8. These references verify the **scope of source documents**, not the factual correctness, originality, copyright clearance, applicability or product implementation of any of the twelve manuscripts. Written, suitably qualified independent editorial assessment remains required.
